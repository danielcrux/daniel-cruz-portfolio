About

A little about how I work.

I design to make people's lives easier. The more complex the product, the more I want it to feel simple.

I'm a Senior Product Designer at Strategy CX, working remotely with U.S. teams on B2B platforms and mobile apps. I work closely with PMs, engineers and stakeholders, and I care as much about why a screen exists as about how it looks.

AI is part of how I work every day. Claude, Cursor and Figma AI help with research synthesis, prototyping and iteration, so cycles get faster without cutting corners on the thinking.

Before product design I spent 4 years in architecture, at Perkins&Will, FGMF and Fernandes Arquitetos, leading projects for clients like Amazon AWS. That's where I learned to think in systems.
